🛠️ SillyTavern, Tavern AI & Character Tavern — Open-Source Roleplay Explained

The Tavern AI ecosystem can be confusing, so here's a quick breakdown. SillyTavern is the most actively maintained open-source frontend for AI roleplay. It evolved from TavernAI (the original project) and supports character cards, conversation branching, group chats, and extensive customization — all with no content restrictions.

Character Tavern AI is a separate platform that hosts pre-made character bots in a simpler interface. It's closer to SpicyChat or CrushOn in its approach but with a smaller community. Yodayo Tavern combines character chat with anime art generation.

If you're coming from Janitor AI or Character AI and want full control, SillyTavern is the upgrade path most advanced users recommend. It pairs well with services like OpenRouter (for cloud LLMs) or KoboldCpp (for running local models). The initial setup takes 15–30 minutes, but the result is the most powerful, private, and uncensored AI roleplay experience available.
