Key findings

Observations from direct testing across all 15 platforms, July 2026.

10×

Pricing range across the market

The cheapest full-access plan (CandyAI annual at $3.99/mo) costs 11× less than the most expensive (JuicyChat monthly at $43.99/mo) — an unusually wide spread for a maturing SaaS category, suggesting the market has not yet converged on standard pricing tiers.

52%

Platforms now include image generation

13 of 25 tracked platforms include NSFW image generation in their premium plans. Image generation has shifted from a premium differentiator to an expected feature at the $10+/month tier, with video generation still scarce at 6 of 25 platforms.

6 only

Video generation remains scarce

Only CandyAI, Joi AI, Secrets AI, Funy AI, LusyChat, ourdream.ai, Dondi AI, Get-Harder AI, and Promptchan offer video generation — all other platforms either do not offer it or offer it as a costly add-on. This is a significant capability gap versus what users expect in 2026.

33 msgs

Average daily free limit

Across platforms offering a message-based free tier, the average daily limit is 33 messages. SpicyChat's 50/day limit is the most generous among mainstream paid-or-free platforms. The majority of newer platforms offer only vague "limited" free trials rather than a defined message count.

$9.99

Median monthly premium price

The median monthly premium price across all 25 platforms is $9.99. This compares to $9.99–$19.99/month for mainstream AI assistants (ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro), placing AI companion apps at rough pricing parity with general-purpose AI despite more specialized and explicit feature sets.

76%

Platforms offer persistent memory

19 of 25 platforms now offer some form of conversation memory or character continuity on premium plans. Memory has become table stakes in 2026 for any platform positioned as a "companion" rather than a chatbot — only image/video-generation-first platforms tend to omit it.

Methodology

All data on this page was collected through direct use and subscription testing of each platform by the xchatbots research team. Pricing data is taken from each platform's own subscription page and verified monthly. Free tier message limits were tested with new accounts. Feature availability (image generation, voice, video, memory) reflects what is actually accessible on each plan, not what is marketed — in several cases, advertised features were found to be unavailable, restricted, or in beta during testing.

Platforms included were selected based on measurable user activity (social mentions, Reddit presence, affiliate traffic) and are not sponsored or paid placements. Xchatbots earns affiliate commissions from some platforms listed; this does not influence the data or findings presented here. For corrections, pricing updates, or press enquiries, contact info@x-chatbots.com.

This page covers the NSFW AI chatbot and AI companion category. For the broader AI assistant market (general-purpose models), this data is not representative. The platforms tracked here are purpose-built for adult or companion use cases and are not comparable to tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini in terms of content policy or intended use.
